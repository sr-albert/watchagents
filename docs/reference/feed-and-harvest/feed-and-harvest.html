Feed & Harvest

Turning the farm's resource props into a token-budget reading — decisions so far, and the one question still open.

Settled

Scope
Whole-farm

One feed store, one harvest pile. No per-project attribution, no new data path.

What feed measures
Tokens, not CPU/RAM

CPU and RAM already drive the animals' own behaviour. Tokens have no presence in the scene at all.

The bale's denominator
A ceiling you configure

Set your plan's budget in the farmhouse; the bale then empties honestly against it.

Open: what does the harvest measure?

With a configured ceiling, remaining is just ceiling minus burnt — so the harvest needs a different job, or it's the same fact drawn twice.

A · A longer clock

Bale = the current 5-hour block. Milk = today's total, across blocks, resetting at midnight.

  • Stock vs yield — the truest farm reading
  • Harvest survives the 5-hour reset
  • Needs a second ccusage daily call
Recommended

B · Money, not tokens

Bale = tokens left in the block. Milk tank = dollars spent, filling toward the projected total.

  • Different unit — cannot be redundant
  • Zero new calls: costUSD and projection.totalCost are already fetched
  • Two units on screen at once to read

C · Work produced

Eggs = outputTokens only — the tokens Claude actually wrote back.

  • The prettiest metaphor: hens lay what the farm made
  • Output is a small, lumpy slice — the tray sits near-empty

D · No harvest

The bale alone. One prop, one fact.

  • Nothing redundant; §7 "no new art" stays intact
  • Loses the sense of accumulation entirely

Facts that shaped this

  • ccusage has no plan quota. blocks -t max adds no field to the JSON; "max" just means your own heaviest recorded block. Any real ceiling has to be configured.
  • CPU is mostly near zero. Claude's work happens server-side (spec §0a), and SessionState.swift:25-35 already spends cpu/mem on the thresholds that make animals stand, walk, or bounce.
  • Per-project attribution was available but not needed. ccusage session --json keys on session UUID, and the UUID resolves to a project through ~/.claude/projects/. Set aside with the whole-farm scope.

Still to decide after this

Where the props stand. The barnyard hay yard, the barn's right shoulder, or the info panel — the scene has an existing farmyard cluster the bale could join.
Spec amendments. §7 compromise 4 rules out a silo and says nothing needs new art but the font; §5.1 ranks cues by how much you must act, and a feed gauge must not out-shout overloaded.
How the ceiling is entered. Plan presets risk going stale; a raw token number defaulted to your observed maximum does not.
